Still records meetings, calls, lectures and voice memos, turns them into a timestamped transcript, and writes up the decisions and action items — entirely on your device.

There is no account and no server doing the thinking. Still transcribes with its own speech model running on your iPhone, iPad or Mac, so recording and transcription work in airplane mode. Still makes no network request until you download the speech model, turn on sync, or share something.

RECORD
• One Record button on iPhone, iPad and Mac
• On the Mac, capture your microphone and the other side of a call as separate tracks
• Record calls automatically: when an app you allow starts using the microphone, Still starts recording, and saves when the call ends
• Start and stop from the menu bar or a keyboard shortcut, without opening the window
• Audio is written in small encrypted pieces as you speak, so a crash costs you seconds rather than the session

READ BACK
• A live transcript while you record, and a full transcript when you stop
• Every line is linked to the moment it was said — tap it and hear it
• A local AI model cleans up the raw transcript: filler words trimmed, punctuation fixed, meaning untouched
• Words Still is unsure of stay marked rather than being rewritten into confident text
• Correct a name and the original stays beside your correction
• Speakers are recognized by name and matched across meetings, so the same person is the same speaker next time — rename a group without touching the transcript

UNDERSTAND
• Summary, decisions and action items, each one quoting the transcript it came from
• Meeting, lecture, interview and voice-note formats, chosen for you and changeable afterwards
• Translation beneath the original, with the original still the record of what was said

CONNECT (Mac)
• Run Still as a local MCP server so Claude Code, Codex or another AI tool on your Mac can read your real meeting notes and decisions — nothing leaves your device to do it

FIND AND KEEP
• Search titles, transcripts, corrections and notes
• Export as PDF, Markdown, plain text, audio, or an encrypted Still archive
• Choose how long audio is kept; deleting is undoable for thirty days

PRIVACY
Your Library is encrypted with a key kept in this device's keychain. If you turn on iCloud sync, Apple stores ciphertext only: no transcript, note, audio or key ever leaves your device in readable form. Diagnostics is off, and reports carry no content even when you turn it on.

Every feature is free.
